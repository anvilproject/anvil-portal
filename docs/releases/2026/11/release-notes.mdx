The AnVIL team is excited to announce the latest quarterly data release. This includes the first release of MOHD study data, expanded datasets for GA4K and DepMap, and initial data from the next phase of eMERGE.

**MOHD v1 Release:** The Multi-Omics for Health and Disease (MOHD) Consortium, a National Institutes of Health (NIH) initiative, has released its first dataset from 956 participants. This deeply-characterized multi-omic dataset reflects a heterogeneous population (including substantial admixture among participants), helping fill gaps in existing genomic research resources.

**GA4K v6 Release:** Genomic Answers for Kids (GA4K), a research initiative of the Children’s Mercy Research Institute, released the sixth version of the data repository, expanding the number of available sequences and analyses by more than 15%. Researchers looking for read-level data at medium to medium-high depth, particularly in pediatric populations or families, may want to apply for access to this unusually large dataset for rare diseases. Highlights from the v6 release include more long-read data, newly-described phenotypes associated with rare disease profiles, and sequencing from recently-enrolled individuals.

**DepMap v4 Release:** The Broad Institute’s Cancer Dependency Map (DepMap) project has released its fourth dataset, expanded by 375 additional cell lines and three new disease-specific consent groups. The v4 release includes underlying data for the NextGen cancer models recently published in Neiswender JV, Maffa S, Brenan L, et al. (2026) in Nature. That study covered new genomic and molecular subtypes, identified new biomarker-associated vulnerabilities, and reported on 10 cancer types: ampullary, biliary tract, brain, breast, colorectal, esophageal-stomach, ovarian, pancreatic, prostate, and uterine.

**eMERGE Phase IV v1 Release:** The eMERGE (electronic MEdical Records and GEnomics) network, a consortium through the National Human Genome Research Institute and U.S. medical research institutions, has released nearly 24,000 genome-informed risk reports combining cross-ancestry polygenic risk, monogenic risk, family history risk, and clinical risk for 11 common conditions, including cancers and cardiovascular disease. The dataset contains raw sequencing data, VCF files, phenotype data, and participant surveys. Approximately half of the participants reflected in the genomic data are individuals not typically involved in research, specifically from groups not well-represented in currently available genome-wide association (GWAS) datasets.

Read on for release notes, study DULs, and access information.

If you are interested in these datasets, please submit data access requests through dbGaP. The datasets are now findable on the [AnVIL Data Explorer]({browserURL}/datasets) for cohort building and on the [AnVIL Data Library](https://duos.org/datalibrary/anvil) for dataset-level search.

For additional questions, please refer to [finding and using AnVIL data](https://support.terra.bio/hc/en-us/articles/34594440468635-Finding-and-using-AnVIL-data) and [AnVIL releases](/releases).
